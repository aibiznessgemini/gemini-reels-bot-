# living_ball: "In this world, the football is alive and chooses who scores." 7 stars, narrator off-screen.
Haaland kicked the ball as hard as he could... and it flew straight back into his face. Bonk!
You see, in this world, the football is alive. And it chooses who scores.
It loves Messi. It follows him everywhere, like a little puppy.
It hates Ronaldo's free kicks. Way too much power. So it just stops in mid-air... and drops.
Mbappé tried to outrun it. Bad idea. The ball ran even faster, the other way.
Neymar tried a step-over to trick it. The ball tricked him back. Down he goes!
Then the youngest one, Yamal, walked up to the ball... and smiled.
And it rolled straight to his feet.
That's when I noticed something. The ball never runs from someone who is smiling.
So all seven of them smiled at it. At the same time.
The ball spun... and spun... It couldn't choose.
So it kicked itself into the net.
Goal of the season? The ball. Again.
