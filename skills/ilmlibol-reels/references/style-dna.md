# Style DNA — what the four reference reels do

Measured from the four reels the owner gave as the model (Uzbek Islamic reminders, vertical).
These are the reasons the videos feel calm, sincere and "made", not templated.

## 1. Format and pace

| Reel | Length | Look | Shots | Avg shot |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pumpkin field — "boylikni Allohdan soʻrash" | 79 s | watercolor 2D | ~25 | 3.2 s |
| Mosque, car, shoes — "neʼmatni esdan chiqarish" | 76 s | watercolor 2D | ~22 | 3.4 s |
| Hadith after the toilet — "hamdu sano" | 69 s | watercolor 2D | ~23 | 3.0 s |
| Endless rope — "oxirat va dunyo" | 54 s | cinematic 3D | ~13 | 4.1 s |

- Vertical 9:16, 24–30 fps. 50–80 seconds. One voice, calm and warm, talking to "siz".
- A new picture roughly every 3 seconds in 2D (17–20 per minute), every 4 seconds in 3D.
- **The camera never stops**: every shot is a slow push-in, pull-out, pan, tilt or crane. Stills
  never sit still.
- 2D reels change shots with **plain cuts**; the continuous drift and the subtitle that carries
  across the cut make it feel smooth. The 3D reel uses long continuous camera moves (macro on the
  rope → pull out → aerial over the whole mahalla) instead of many cuts.

## 2. Picture follows the words, literally

Every phrase is illustrated by the thing it names:

| Words (Uzbek) | Picture |
|---|---|
| "Ha, musulmon, boy boʻlgani…" | man hoeing a pumpkin field, water flowing into the furrow |
| "boylikni Allohdan soʻrash kerak" | the same man, from behind, standing among ripe pumpkins, looking at the horizon |
| "…panoh tilash kerak, lekin shu…" | the same field, sky turns to storm, rain begins |
| "duolaringizdan Alloh qanaqa rozi…" | close on big pumpkins shining in the rain |
| "tavfiq" | the man bends and picks up a pumpkin |
| "Mana hozir har joydan keldinglar-a" | mosque yard from the road, cars arriving, people walking in |
| "kim adirdan keldi" | a car on a winding road through hills |
| "Piyoda ketishni tasavvur qiling" | the man walking alone on a hot empty road, sun overhead |
| "Mashinaning neʼmatligini esdan chiqarganmiz" | the man washing his white car in the street |
| "Toʻppa-toʻgʻri aytyapman" | close on the clean windscreen with sky reflection |
| "Chiqsangiz oyoq kiyim turganligi neʼmat emasmi?" | the mosque shoe rack, then the man taking his shoes |
| "Paygʻambarimiz ﷺ qazoyi hojatdan chiqib…" | no person at all: clay jug pouring water, a garden pergola |
| "Menga aziyat yetkazadiganini mendan ketkazgan" | a stream carrying fallen leaves away |
| "menga foyda qiladigan narsani qoldirgan" | trees, irrigation channels, then young plants growing |
| "Bir arqonni tasavvur qiling" | a white rope lying across grass at dusk |
| "ikkinchi uchi ketgan… mahallalarni oralab" | the rope running out of the yard, through streets, across the whole district from the air |
| "10 santimetr boshida izolenta bor" | macro: blue tape on the rope, "10 sm" measurement bracket |
| "cheksiz ip" | the rope to the horizon, "∞" mark |

Lessons:
- **Concrete nouns get shown**; the viewer always sees what they hear.
- **Abstract ideas become everyday Uzbek life** (blessing = shoes in a rack, a car, water).
- **Metaphors are built visually** step by step (rope → tape → infinity).
- **Hadith = no people**; symbolic objects and nature carry it.
- **Weather is the emotion**: dusk calm → storm for trial → rain-washed shine for mercy; pink
  blossoms for countless blessings; soft daylight for gratitude.

## 3. Places and blocks

A reel uses only 2–5 places, and spends 3–8 shots in each from different angles:
pumpkin field (wide, back view, rain, close pumpkins, harvest, walking away) → mosque/car/shoes.
This repetition with variation is what makes it feel like one film and not a slideshow.

## 4. The 2D look (watercolor village)

- Hand-drawn **watercolor and ink on paper**: thin dark outlines, soft washes, visible paper grain,
  blotchy watercolor sky.
- **Uzbek setting**: mud-brick and plastered walls, flat roofs, poplar rows (terak), mulberry and
  fruit trees, irrigation ariqs, tapchan/soʻri, clay jugs (koʻza), mosques with a green/teal dome,
  hills in the distance, old white cars.
- **Palette**: sand, ochre, sage green, olive, dusty rose and peach sky, teal dome, slate grey for
  storms, pink blossom. Low saturation, no pure black, no neon.
- **Hero**: a man in a white doʻppi, muted jacket (grey-blue or green), grey trousers, simple
  face often unseen (shown from behind, side or far away). A woman, when present, in a long green
  dress and white headscarf.
- **Effects** drawn over the picture: rain streaks, splashes, ripples on water, falling petals,
  sun haze.

## 5. The 3D look (cinematic dusk)

- Stylised 3D render, **dusk**: green-teal sky with a warm peach horizon, warm street lamps,
  long shadows, lit windows.
- Mahalla from above (grid of houses, walls, trees), grass in macro, a single object as the
  metaphor carrier.
- Small rounded **labels** appear on screen for measurements ("10 sm") and a "∞" mark.

## 6. Text on screen

- Subtitles in the lower third (≈ 78 % down in 2D, ≈ 74 % in 3D), **word by word**: each word fades
  in as it is spoken; the phrase stays until the next phrase starts.
- 2D: dark green semibold text on a **soft frosted-white glass pill** that blurs the picture behind.
  3D: white bold text with a soft shadow, up to two lines, no pill.
- The **key word** of a phrase in warm amber/orange (shukrini, hamdu, tavfiq, rozi, cheksiz,
  10 santimetr).
- A small signature under the subtitle on every frame — for us **ilmlibol**.
- No title card, no end screen, no intro logo: the reel starts with the first word.

## 7. Sound

Voice only (sometimes a very soft ambience). The user's audio already contains everything; we
never add music or effects.
