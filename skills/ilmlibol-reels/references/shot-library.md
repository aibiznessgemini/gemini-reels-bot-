# Shot library — turning a phrase into a picture

For every phrase ask, in this order:
1. **Does it name something visible?** Show exactly that, in our world, being used.
2. **Is it an instruction to imagine?** Change the world of the previous shot to the imagined one
   (same hero, new situation).
3. **Is it a feeling or a value?** Find the everyday Uzbek moment where that value lives.
4. **Is it a quote (hadith, dua, scholar)?** Symbolic: place, object, nature, hands — no faces.
5. **Is it a number, size, time span?** 3D style: show the object and put a `label`; 2D style:
   show many vs. one (a full shelf vs. one pair, a field vs. one fruit).

Then decide the **framing** by where the phrase is in its thought:

| Moment in the thought | Framing | Move |
|---|---|---|
| First line of a new idea / new place | wide establishing, hero small | `push_in` or `crane_down` |
| Explaining, describing | medium, hero from behind or side | `pan_left` / `pan_right` / `drift` |
| The key word, the punch | close detail of the object or hands | `push_in` / `push_in_fast` |
| A question to the viewer ("…emasmi?") | close on the object, or hero turning to look | `push_in_slow` |
| Turning to Alloh, duo, hamd | sky, light, hero looking up | `tilt_up` / `crane_up` |
| End of a thought, conclusion | wide again, hero walks away or the world opens | `pull_out` |
| Final line of the reel | the widest, brightest shot | `pull_out` + `dip_white` |

## Recipes by theme

**Blessings (neʼmat) of daily life**
- shoes waiting in the mosque rack · water from a jug into a bowl · bread from a tandir · a
  child asleep · a working tap · a car washed clean · light switching on in a room · a full
  dastarkhan · healthy hands at work · eyes looking at the sky (from behind).

**Rizq, wealth, poverty**
- a man hoeing a field, water flowing into the furrow · ripe harvest under rain · an empty
  pocket / empty bowl (close) · coins on a palm · a small shop opening at dawn.

**Trial, misfortune (musibat), warning**
- the same place under a storm: grade `storm` + `rain`; wind bending poplars; a cracked jug; a
  dry ariq; a closed door; a man sitting with head down (from behind).

**Mercy, forgiveness, hope**
- rain stopping, light breaking through clouds (`sun_rays`); a sprout in cracked earth; blossom
  on a bare branch; ripples on calm water; a lamp lit in a dark window.

**Gratitude (shukr), praise (hamd)**
- hero on a prayer mat facing a window; hands raised in duo (from behind); birds over the
  village; a wide sunrise over fields.

**Time, death, the hereafter**
- a clock; an hourglass; sun setting behind poplars; footprints ending; an empty chair on a soʻri;
  a road to the horizon; a graveyard edge with no inscriptions; leaves falling.

**Knowledge, a scholar's lesson**
- a lamp over an open book with unreadable script; a teacher and student walking (from behind);
  a seed becoming a tree across 3 shots.

**Family**
- mother pouring tea (no face detail, modest); father and son walking to the mosque; an elder's
  hands; children at a doorway; shoes of a whole family at the door.

## Hadith and quotes

- "Paygʻambarimiz ﷺ … dedilar" → a place of that time or a calm symbolic object; never a person
  standing for the Prophet ﷺ.
- Inside the quote, illustrate the **meaning** with nature: "harmful thing taken away" → a stream
  carrying leaves away; "useful thing left" → green plants growing along the channel.
- Close the quote on a calm wide (garden, sky), then return to the hero for the speaker's
  explanation.

## Building a metaphor across shots (the 3D rope pattern)

When the speaker builds an image ("imagine a rope… one end here… the other end never ends…
10 cm has tape… compare 10 cm to infinity"):
1. Introduce the object close and simple.
2. Follow it outward in 2–3 shots, each wider (yard → street → whole district from the air).
3. Mark the small part (macro + `labels` with bracket).
4. Pull out to the full scale (horizon, `∞` label).
5. Return to the small part for the conclusion.

## Continuity checklist for a block

- Same place, same time of day (unless the words change it), same hero clothes.
- Vary the camera distance every shot: wide → medium → close → wide.
- Vary the move every shot.
- Weather changes only on a meaning change, and then it stays for the block.
- Keep 2–5 places in a whole reel; return to the first place at the end if the talk returns to its
  opening idea.
